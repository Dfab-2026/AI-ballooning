# Line-touch ballooning

- Normal balloon circle size is preserved.
- Visible leader is approximately 1 cm by default.
- Arrowheads terminate exactly on a real engineering/dimension/extension/leader/feature line.
- Measurement digits, tolerances, symbols, and words are protected no-touch zones.
- The backend post-processes AI target coordinates and snaps them to nearby straight CAD linework when possible.
- Balloon placement considers all four diagonal directions so top or bottom can be used depending on available space.
- Manual balloon targets remain user-selected and the arrowhead terminates exactly at the clicked point.
