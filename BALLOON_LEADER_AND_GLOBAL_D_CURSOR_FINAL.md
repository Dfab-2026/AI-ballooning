# Final balloon leader + global D cursor update

- Drawing balloon leader distance is increased by about another 0.5 cm.
- A minimum leader distance is enforced in the browser, so older/cached analysis also displays the longer leader.
- New AI balloon placement and manual Add Balloon placement use the larger offset by default.
- Arrow-side measurement clearance is unchanged; extra length is added from the balloon-circle side.
- The whole application uses one D-balloon cursor. The arrow tip is the clickable hotspot at the upper-left and the D circle is on the opposite/lower-right side.
- Export behavior and Excel/report behavior are unchanged.
